# CTET Sessions (2020 ke baad)

Har session ka folder mein `meta.json` hai — dates, edition, mode, answer-key info aur links.

**Note:** Calendar year 2020 mein koi CTET conduct nahi hua (pandemic); July 2020 wala exam
31 Jan 2021 ko hua. Isi tarah Dec 2023 wala exam 21 Jan 2024 ko, aur Dec 2025 wala exam
7–8 Feb 2026 ko hua. 2025 mein koi session nahi hua.

## Real question papers kahan se milein ge

Actual CTET Paper-I booklets aur official answer keys CBSE ke copyrighted material hain —
isi liye unhe yahan copy nahi kiya gaya. Original PDFs yahan se download karo:

1. **Official:** https://ctet.nic.in/ (archive/previous year papers section)
2. CareerPower — year-wise Paper 1 & 2 PDFs (2018–2026) + answer keys:
   https://www.careerpower.in/ctet-previous-year-question-paper.html
3. Adda247 — Paper 1 & 2 PDFs with solutions (2024 ke sets codes ke saath):
   https://www.adda247.com/teaching-jobs-exam/ctet-previous-year-question-paper-pdfs/
4. ExamCart — 2024 & 2026 sets (Set E/C, Code H etc.):
   https://examcart.in/blogs/central-exam/ctet-previous-year-question-paper-paper-1-2-out-download-pdf

## Section-wise breakdown (Paper-I booklet)

Real booklet mein sections aise hote hain:

1. **Child Development and Pedagogy** — Q1–30 (Hindi + English dono versions same numbering)
2. **Language I** — Q31–60 (booklet mein har language ka alag block: Hindi, Sanskrit, English, ...)
3. **Language II** — Q61–90 (har language ka alag block)
4. **Mathematics** — Q91–120
5. **Environmental Studies** — Q121–150

Candidate apni chuni hui Language-I aur Language-II ke blocks hi answer karta hai.
