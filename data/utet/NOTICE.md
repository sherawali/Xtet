# UTET source attribution and scope

The files under `data/utet/bank-v2/` are a scoped, unmodified snapshot of [`sherawali/tet`](https://github.com/sherawali/tet) at commit `6792637052eb0bcddd27b9290ddd082faa725a4d`.

Attribution: **TET Question Bank contributors (2026)**. License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). This project copies the UTET Paper-I question/form/appearance/stimulus data, schemas, source manifests, and contributor audit rows into a new relational format. Changes are format/schema conversion and added provenance; the upstream question text is not corrected by this pipeline.

The upstream license says the bank is independently authored, is not affiliated with or endorsed by UBSE/CBSE or another exam authority, and is not derived verbatim from an examination authority. Its answer/review flags are contributor assertions, not proof of an official UTET final answer key. The database therefore classifies imported UTET answer values as provisional source-repository answers and excludes them from the official-final-answer view.

CTET source files in the repository do not have an inspected root license statement. This notice does not change their licensing or grant redistribution rights.
