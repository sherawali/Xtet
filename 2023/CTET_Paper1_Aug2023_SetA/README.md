## Notes

- Is booklet (Set A) me sirf English aur Hindi dono languages ke questions hain - Part IV me Eng-1 + Hindi-1, Part V me Eng-2 + Hindi-2. In 7 files me poori booklet aa jaati hai.
- Sanskrit-1 / Sanskrit-2 is paper me NAHI hain: CBSE ke instructions ke anusar Sanskrit, Urdu jaisi baaki languages alag "Supplement (Language) Test Booklet" me aati hain, main booklet me nahi. Isliye is Set A se Sanskrit ke questions nahi nikal sakte.
- Hindi-1 me gadyansh (Q106-114) aur kavyansh (Q115-120), Eng-1 me Mizoram solo-trip ka passage (Q106-114) aur "Gather ye rosebuds" kavita (Q115-120) shamil hain. Hindi-2 me 2 gadyansh (Q121-127, Q128-135) aur Eng-2 me 2 passages (Q121-127, Q128-135) shamil hain.
- Q91-105 ka pedagogy part Hindi-1 aur Eng-1 me same topics ke translations hain; waise hi Q136-150 Hindi-2 aur Eng-2 me.
- Questions bilkul paper jaisa rakha gaya hai - bina kisi extra sign (jaise *) ke.
